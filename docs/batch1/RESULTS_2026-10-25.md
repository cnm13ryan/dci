## 5. Per configuration

A configuration is model × wording frame × B limit, 16 in all.

**How the populations nest.** The populations are of reference instructions
that can coexist with B, measured against A (however × 6):

- **G6:** every eligible, compatible reference.
- **G4:** those of the same operation as A (counting a target word).
- **G2:** those of the same operation and object type (a target word).
- **G1, G3, G5:** the subsets of G2, G4 and G6 matched to A, that is, within
  ±0.10 of A's compliance in that model-frame (D10).

**The contrasts.** Each one needs:

- **P1:** G1 with at least 3 distinct words, and G2 adding at least 2.
- **P2:** G3 adding at least 2 of another object type.
- **P3:** G5 adding at least 3 of another operation (D11, minimum 3), with
  G3 holding at least 3.

**Extra columns.**

- *Stage 1 only*: the support when only Stage 1 topics are used.
- *Support share in resamples*: the share of 200 topic-cluster bootstrap
  resamples (D14) in which the contrast is supported.

**Why it is infeasible.** Difficulty matching is the binding filter.

- **Before matching:** 15-18 references share A's operation and object (G2).
- **After matching:** 1-5 remain matched to A (G1).
- **By model:**
  - Qwen3-4B has a single matched word in all four of its configurations.
  - Qwen3-4B-Instruct has a single matched word under frame F0.
  - Gemma 3n E4B supports P1 in all four of its configurations.

  type.
- **P3** reaches its minimum of 3 additional other-operation references once
  (Gemma 3n E2B, F0, B 10).

## 8. What the data point to (for the researcher's revision; no choice is made here)

These are observations, not proposals. Each would be a new decision with its
own review, and a revised design would need a fresh pilot on topics that
have not been seen.

- **Matching (D10)** is where references are lost: from 15-18 (G2) to 1-5
  (G1). The disclosed noise of ±0.10 matching (about 45% misclassification
  within 2 points of the edge) applies here.
- **The A count (D08)** at × 6 is strongly frame-sensitive. That moves A's
  difficulty away from the references in some model-frames.
- **The library (D01)** has no other object types near A's difficulty, which
  is what P2 needs, and few other-operation references near it, which is what
  P3 needs.

## 9. Limitations

1. **Freeze 2 validates on Linux, not on macOS.** Re-validated on the Mac
   from the verified copy at the same commit, Freeze 1 and every part of
   Freeze 2 reproduce except `calibration_hash`. Four reported confidence-
   interval bounds (`diff_ci` for `unq.bigram` and `ew.although.4`, in four
   configurations) differ between the platforms in the 15th-17th
   significant digit, for example −0.144418286145479 on the host against
   −0.14441828614547902 on the Mac. No decision depends on them: matching
   uses exact rational arithmetic, and every selection, population and
   verdict is identical. But the hash includes the floats. Validate this
   Freeze 2 on Linux at `0cde3b9`, where it reproduces with no problems. For
   the next pilot, the hashed calibration should be made
   platform-independent; that is not changed here, because doing so would
   void this run's Freeze 1.
2. **Regeneration is not byte-exact.** About 95-97% of rows regenerated in
   a fresh process are identical (`PILOT_ABORT_2026-10-25.md`). The evidence
   is the stored rows bound by Freeze 2.
3. **The post-audit fixes were not re-audited before Freeze 1.** The third
   audit's fixes and the later D14 and D12 commits (`9b77370..0cde3b9`) went
   to Freeze 1 at the researcher's instruction, before the auditor's
   re-check of that delta.
4. **Approvals were transcribed by the implementing agent,** at the
   researcher's explicit instruction ("I give all approvals"; D12
   re-approved with "i approve" after the amendment). The researcher's brief
   had reserved this for him.
5. **Pilot topics only.** These are 96 topics: 32 in Stage 1 and 64 in
   Stage 2, refined cells only. The 480 reserved topics were not used, as
   designed.
6. **Not yet durable evidence.** `runs/` is gitignored, and the verified
   copy lives outside the repository. Promote it before citing these results.

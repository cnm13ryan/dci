**Stability analysis.** A read-only analysis on the instance at `8a9422f`
(`../batch1-v2-stability-analysis-2026-10-25/analysis/v2-stability-2026-10-25/REPORT.md`)
reproduces Freeze 2 exactly. It reports the following.

- **Feasible share per contrast** across the resamples: P1 0.90, P2 0.235, P3 0.47. P2 fails in 146 of the 163 INFEASIBLE resamples.
- **The target effectively counts model-frames.** B8 and B10 agree in every resample, and Qwen-Instruct F1 never counts. So "12 of 16" is in effect 6 of 7 usable model-frames.
- **The marginal P2 and P3 cells rest on the minimum number of references,** each sitting near ±0.10.
- **The main cause is single-draw matching noise.** Matched differences have 95% intervals about ±0.13–0.14 wide on 96 topics, wider than the tolerance.
- **More topics would not help.** A nested bootstrap at 96–960 T2 topics gives P(FEASIBLE) 0.12–0.17. P2's best estimate is about 10 of 16, so P2 is likely short in truth.

These are that analysis's findings. This note reproduces the headline numbers from Freeze 2, but not the analysis itself.

## Why this result is provisional

1. **The D14 flags block** (above).
2. **Audit B1** (`../audit-batch1-v2-2026-10-26/AUDIT_REPORT.md`). The carry rule measured only 110 of the 161 references on T2, chosen for plausible matching to A. G2, G4 and G6 therefore come from a matched-leaning subset, which shrinks P1's contrast.
3. **Approved items not implemented in v2** (audit M1): the C′ top-up, the R5(a) sensitivities and the T1-versus-T2 agreement check.
4. **Decision texts.** The v2 decision texts described v1 (audit M2). The rules executed were v2's, bound by the approved payload hashes.
5. **Known CLI crashes.** `select-refinement` and `finalize-calibration` exited 1 after writing their outputs (a v1 assumption in the summary print and in the readable report). No evidence was affected. `reference_selection.md` was not written on the instance; the report fix is `50e0b7d`.

## What follows

## Family semantics

- `adjacent_shared_word` (relational_dependency / sentence_pair, verifier `b1.adjacent_shared_word`, forced_production): at least {min_sentences} sentences; each adjacent pair shares a folded word of at least {min_letters} letters
- `alphabetical_openers` (ordering / sentence, verifier `b1.alphabetical_openers`, forced_production): at least {min_sentences} sentences whose folded first words are strictly increasing in code-point order
- `chain_letter` (relational_dependency / sentence_pair, verifier `b1.chain_letter`, forced_production): at least {min_sentences} sentences; each later sentence's first letter equals the last letter of the previous sentence's last word
- `chain_word` (relational_dependency / sentence_pair, verifier `b1.chain_word`, forced_production): at least {min_sentences} sentences; each later sentence's first word equals the previous sentence's last word
- `distinct_openers` (uniqueness / sentence, verifier `b1.distinct_openers`, forced_production): at least {min_sentences} sentences, all first words distinct
- `exact_char_count` (exact_count / punctuation, verifier `b1.exact_char_count`, forced_production): the raw character {char!r} occurs exactly {n} times
- `exact_numeral_count` (exact_count / numeral, verifier `b1.exact_numeral_count`, forced_production): exactly {n} numbers written as digits occur
- `exact_paragraph_count` (exact_count / paragraph, verifier `b1.exact_paragraph_count`, forced_production): the answer has exactly {n} blank-line-separated paragraphs
- `exact_sentence_count` (exact_count / sentence, verifier `b1.exact_sentence_count`, forced_production): the answer has exactly {n} sentences
- `exact_word_count` (exact_count / word, verifier `b1.exact_word_count`, forced_production): the number of words equal to '{word}' (folded) is exactly {n}
- `forbid_letter` (prohibition / letter, verifier `b1.forbid_letter`, min_words): no word contains the letter '{letter}' in either case
- `forbid_words` (prohibition / word, verifier `b1.forbid_words`, min_words): none of {words} occurs as a whole word
- `include_words` (required_inclusion / word, verifier `b1.include_words`, forced_production): every one of {words} occurs at least once as a whole word
- `length_order` (ordering / sentence, verifier `b1.length_order`, forced_production): at least {min_sentences} sentences whose word counts are strictly {direction}
- `max_word_length` (numerical_bound / word, verifier `b1.max_word_length`, min_words): every word has at most {m} letters
- `no_repeated_bigrams` (uniqueness / word_sequence, verifier `b1.no_repeated_bigrams`, min_words): no pair of consecutive folded words (over the whole answer) occurs twice
- `no_repeated_words` (uniqueness / word, verifier `b1.no_repeated_words`, min_words): no folded word of at least {min_letters} letters occurs twice
- `positional_question` (positional / answer_boundary, verifier `b1.positional_question`, forced_production): the answer ends with a question mark
- `positional_word` (positional / answer_boundary, verifier `b1.positional_word`, forced_production): the answer's {where} word (folded) is '{word}'
- `sentence_word_bound` (numerical_bound / sentence, verifier `b1.sentence_word_bound`, min_words): every sentence has {bound} {k} words

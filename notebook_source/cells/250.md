## 16. Stage 1 — screen the unrelated targets, then GATE 1

Admission needs resolved dynamic range in two places: in isolation, and when
the target is one of two constraints. v13 decided both from point estimates, so
a candidate sitting on a band edge with a wide interval was classified anyway.

v14 classifies a candidate only when its interval lies wholly inside or wholly
outside the band. A candidate whose interval straddles an edge is
**indeterminate**, and if indeterminates are what stand between the bank and
three eligible targets per family, the status is `U_PRECISION_INSUFFICIENT` —
more screening clusters, not a wider band. Only when the classification is
resolved and still short does it become `U_BANK_NOT_IDENTIFIED`.

Bridge targets are screened on the same criteria but assigned only to
validation and test.

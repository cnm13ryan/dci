### 12b. Measurement-validity machinery

The audit sample is built deterministically from the curated response bank, the
synthetic corpus and the mutation set, so **Gate M needs no GPU and no model**.
That is what makes it possible to establish verifier validity before a verifier
is allowed to shape the reference architecture.

Reviewers see the rule being judged and the response. They do not see the
experimental arm, the condition, the automatic verdict, or any identifier that
reveals treatment.

Three changes from v13's reliability review. False-fail error now enters the
gate rather than being computed and ignored — the primary outcome is failure,
so a verifier that invents failures corrupts the estimand exactly as one that
misses them does. Both rates are bounded by Wilson intervals rather than point
estimates. And the audit size is derived from the claimed error ceiling: 12
cases with zero errors bound the true rate at about 0.22, so 12 is a minimum
audit size and not evidence of a 5% error rate.

## 5. Constraint registry, unrelated-target bank, and task splits

Three additions to v13.

**Declared logical relations.** Every constraint records what it is
incompatible with and what it entails, so hard eligibility can be evaluated
without inspecting behaviour. Duplication is additionally detected empirically
from verifier agreement.

**A bridge validation family.** The lexical, format and numerical unrelated
targets are all whole-answer constraints, while $A$ is sentence-structured, so
nothing in v13 tested transport into $A$'s own scope. The bridge family is
sentence-scoped and therefore *does* share $A$'s segmentation span. That is
exactly why it is admissible only for validation and test and never for
building the features that define comparability.

Its absence of a direct $A$ or $B$ pathway is asserted behaviourally rather
than in prose. Each bridge target must be invariant to appending a declared
neutral sentence (so it cannot be reading the total sentence count, which is
$A$) and invariant to reversing sentence order (so it cannot be comparing
across sentences, which is $B$). A candidate failing either probe is rejected
at import.

**A disjoint pilot topic bank.** Inspecting pilot outcomes must not be able to
affect the study design, so the pilot profile draws from its own topics.

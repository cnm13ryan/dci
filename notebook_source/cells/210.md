## 12. The reference-model machinery

Every decision rule is a pure function defined here, so Gate 0 can exercise all
of them on fixtures with a known answer before any of them sees a generation.

Four changes from v13.

**Wording instability is decomposed.** One scalar mean could hide a single
unstable dimension behind three stable ones. v14 computes isolated-difficulty,
outgoing-interference and incoming-vulnerability instability separately, matches
on all three, and reports the maximum as a summary. All three are on the
probability scale, so no normalisation is needed to compare them.

**Sensitivity is estimated per rotation and per order.** v13 pooled one slope
per family across held-out targets and both orders, so a pooled average could
make a caliper wider than the caliper implied by a clearly more sensitive
component. v14 estimates each rotation and order separately, reports the
heterogeneity, and takes the **largest credible upper sensitivity** unless the
spread is demonstrably small. Slope uncertainty additionally uses a
leave-one-constraint-out jackknife, because pairwise differences that share a
constraint are not independent observations.

**Precision protects contenders, not the median.** The relevant question is
whether the candidates that could actually enter the panel are measured well
enough, so the rule now evaluates every contender and takes the worst.

**Hard eligibility is defined without reference to $B$.** Satisfiability,
declared incompatibility, entailment and duplication against both $A$ and $B$,
verifier validity, scope, usable measurements and adequate precision. This is
the universe the pseudo-focal cohort is drawn from, and none of its criteria
mentions resemblance to $B$.

# Directional Constraint Interference v14

Target $A$ = exact sentence count. Focal $B$ = strictly increasing sentence
lengths. Primary estimand: the $6 \times 2$ matrix of order-specific
individual-reference contrasts

$$\tau_{j,o} = \mathbb{E}_X\left[\mu_A(B, X, o) - \mu_A(C_j, X, o)\right],
\qquad o \in \{\text{target\_first}, \text{added\_first}\}$$

on the **failure** scale, with one common panel of exactly six individually
admissible references surviving in both orders, or non-identification.

## What v14 changes and why

v13 implemented the design correctly in its architecture and incorrectly in
six places where the notebook could still reach a confident answer it had not
earned. Every change below closes one of those.

| # | v13 could... | v14 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **validate the wrong estimand.** Gate 3 tuned across $g_0$, $g_1$, $g_2$ and let the best performer authorize confirmation, but the declared primary estimand uses the six individually admissible $g_1$ references. | $g_1$ must pass Gate 3 on its own. $g_0$ is a benchmark and falsification comparator that never authorizes. $g_2$ is fitted and validated separately; its failure withholds only the stochastic secondary estimand. |
| 2 | **validate against itself.** Pseudo-focals and their donors were drawn from $B$'s own eligible neighbourhood, so the test asked whether constraints resembling $B$ predict each other. | A hard-eligible pseudo-focal universe defined by criteria that never mention $B$: satisfiability, entailment and duplication against both $A$ and $B$, verifier validity, scope, usable measurements, adequate precision. Cohort and donor union frozen from $U_{\rm build}$ **before** $U_{\rm val}$ is generated. |
| 3 | **pass on point estimates.** Gates 3 and 4 computed intervals and then decided from point estimates. | Every validation criterion uses the task-cluster bootstrap and an explicit one-sided or equivalence bound. Uncertainty counts against validation. $p > .05$ is never validation. |
| 4 | **hide a bad reference in an average.** Validation was on the panel mean, so $+0.20$ and $-0.20$ cancelled. | Per-reference validation over $C_j \times U_{\rm val} \times \text{order}$ with simultaneous bounds and a catastrophic-mismatch rule. The pooled residual is a secondary diagnostic. |
| 5 | **use unaudited measurement.** The paraphrase audit was an export label and the verifier reliability review ran after selection. | Both are gates before empirical generation. An unsigned audit stops the pipeline. A signed audit can never be overwritten. Verifier validity is established before a verifier is allowed to shape the reference architecture. |
| 6 | **confuse imprecision with absence of support.** One `NotIdentified` covered both. | `PRECISION_INSUFFICIENT` (cannot measure comparability well enough) and `NOT_IDENTIFIED_SUPPORT` (measurement adequate, fewer than six admissible references exist) are separate machine-readable statuses with separate remedies. |

Six further corrections: held-out wordings are now actually evaluated
(Gate 3b); wording instability is decomposed rather than averaged into one
scalar; the precision gate protects the candidates that could actually enter
the panel rather than the pool median; coupling $\kappa$ carries an uncertainty
model instead of being silently dropped when undefined; sensitivity slopes are
estimated per rotation and order with a conservative maximum rather than pooled;
and sample-size planning simulates the estimator that is actually used.

Operationally: three pinned Qwen profiles with hard isolation between them,
Google Drive as the durable source of truth with a verified restore path, and a
benchmark mode that measures throughput instead of guessing it.

## The gate sequence

| Gate | Question | Statuses it can return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0** Implementation | Does the pipeline behave correctly on synthetic data with a known answer? | `PASS` / implementation failure |
| **M** Measurement validity | Are the verifiers and the paraphrase bank valid *before* they shape anything? | `PASS` / `MEASUREMENT_VALIDITY_FAILED` |
| **1** $U$ bank | Do enough unrelated targets have resolved dynamic range? | `PASS` / `U_PRECISION_INSUFFICIENT` / `U_BANK_NOT_IDENTIFIED` |
| **2** Feasibility | Can $B$ be given six individually admissible references in both orders? | `PASS` / `PRECISION_INSUFFICIENT` / `NOT_IDENTIFIED_SUPPORT` |
| **3** Reference validation | Does $g_1$ predict held-out generic burden, per reference, better than arbitrary controls? | `PASS` / `REFERENCE_VALIDATION_FAILED` / `REFERENCE_VALIDATION_NOT_EVALUABLE` |
| **3b** Wording validation | Does that comparability survive wordings never used to build it? | `PASS` / `WORDING_VALIDATION_FAILED` |
| **4** Falsification | Did the frozen architecture generalise to $U_{\rm test}$, per reference? | `PASS` / `FINAL_FALSIFICATION_FAILED` |

Gates 0 through 3b cost no confirmatory compute. Gate 4 is terminal: a failure
is reported with the confirmatory matrix and never repaired inside v14.

## Preserved from v13 without change

$A$ and $B$ as defined above; both orders co-primary; one common panel; exactly
six or non-identification; pool spread never sets a caliper; calipers from the
relationship between calibration mismatch and outcome-relevant generic burden;
uncertainty makes admission stricter and never widens a caliper; $\mathbf{g}_Q$
and $\mathbf{r}_Q$ as directional profiles; response length and other
post-treatment output properties excluded from primary matching;
$U_{\rm build}$, $U_{\rm val}$, $U_{\rm test}$ disjoint with $U_{\rm test}$
terminal; synthetic weighting only after individual admissibility; per-reference
contrasts primary and the stochastic-reference effect secondary; the
least-favourable-reference simultaneous claim rule; and dry-run bypasses that
exist only under `dry_run=True`.

## What is evidence

Only the `study` profile produces scientific evidence. `dev_fast` output is
stamped `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY` and `pilot` output `PILOT_MODEL_SPECIFIC`; neither
can authorize a freeze, and no behavioural result transfers between profiles.
Every empirical gate is rerun from scratch on the study model.

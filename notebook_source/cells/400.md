## 30. Export, manifest, and departures

The archive is written into the export directory and named for its status, so
there is no path by which an unlabelled confirmatory bundle exists while a gate
is open or while the profile is not the study profile.

Everything needed to reconstruct the run lives under the durable root: the
configuration, both audit artifacts, every design and batch plan, every gate
record, the per-batch journal, the scores, the figures, the manifests and the
checkpoints.

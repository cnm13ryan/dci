## 23. GATE 3b — held-out wording validation

v13 reserved paraphrases 6 and 7 for wording validation and then never
evaluated them, so the reservation bought nothing.

Gate 3b measures comparability again for $B$ and the selected panel under those
two wordings, on their own task split, and asks whether the comparability
established under the primary wordings survives. It deliberately measures the
calibration signature and **not** the $A$ outcome: the held-out wordings must
never enter the primary estimand, and computing a $\tau$-like quantity before
the freeze would be the pre-confirmatory peek the design forbids.

The primary estimand remains an average over the prespecified primary
wordings. This gate evaluates transport beyond that finite set; it does not
widen it.

Two wordings cannot support a wording-instability SD, so the criterion runs on
difficulty, outgoing interference and incoming vulnerability. Coupling is
wording-independent and is not re-measured.

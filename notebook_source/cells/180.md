## 9. Prompts, design construction, and the generation budget

Order labels are shared between calibration and confirmation on purpose.
`target_first` always means *the constraint whose failure is being measured is
listed first*. So $g_{Q,u,o}$ and $\tau_{j,o}$ are measured at the same physical
arrangement, which is what makes sensitivity estimated on $U_{\rm build}$
transferable to the confirmatory contrast.

One $k=2$ generation supplies both directional features: with $u$ and $Q$ in the
prompt, $u$'s verdict gives outgoing interference $g$ and $Q$'s verdict gives
incoming vulnerability $r$.

Two new designs. The **wording-validation** design re-measures comparability for
$B$ and the selected panel under the held-out wordings on their own task split,
which is what Gate 3b consumes. The **benchmark** design uses frozen
non-scientific prompts and never enters any calibration or confirmation.

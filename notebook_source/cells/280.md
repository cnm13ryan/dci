## 19. GATE 2 — feasibility: six individually admissible references, both orders

Order-specific components are separate caliper rows, so a candidate that
matches at one position and fails at the other is rejected rather than averaged
into admissibility.

Reaching this cell means precision was already sufficient, so a shortfall here
is `NOT_IDENTIFIED_SUPPORT`: the measurements are good enough and the
references do not exist. Calipers are not widened, $J$ is not lowered, and no
failed natural control is replaced by a hand-picked one.

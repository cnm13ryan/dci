## 14. GATE M — measurement validity, before anything scientific

v13 ran the verifier reliability review after the reference architecture had
been built and used, and treated the paraphrase audit as an export label. Both
are inputs to every downstream decision, so both are gates here.

Gate M requires two things. Every wording this notebook renders has been
approved by a reviewer who did not see model performance. And every verifier
that can determine a scientific quantity meets its reliability criteria, with
**both** error rates bounded above rather than estimated: a verifier that
invents failures corrupts a failure-scale estimand exactly as much as one that
misses them.

Neither needs a GPU. That is the point: measurement validity can be settled
before a single scientific generation is spent.

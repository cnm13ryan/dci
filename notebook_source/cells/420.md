# Interpretation rules

1. **Read the matrix, not a scalar.** The primary result is $\tau_{j,o}$ at two
   positions. If the orders differ in sign, say so first; the 50/50 average
   answers a different question.
2. **The reference-robust claim has one rule.** The simultaneous max-$T$ lower
   bound on the least favourable admitted reference must exceed zero at that
   order.
3. **A wide interval covering zero is not evidence of absence.** Only an
   interval inside the SESOI band supports absence.
4. **The estimand names its reference model.** No sentence of the form "$B$
   interferes with $A$" is supported without naming $g_1$, the panel, the scope
   restriction and the caliper rule. $g_0$ answers "versus a random admissible
   constraint"; $g_2$ answers "versus a prespecified stochastic reference
   intervention". These are different questions and must not be collapsed.
5. **Distinguish the stopping statuses.** `PRECISION_INSUFFICIENT` means
   comparability could not be measured well enough and more calibration tasks
   would help. `NOT_IDENTIFIED_SUPPORT` means measurement was adequate and six
   admissible references do not exist. `REFERENCE_VALIDATION_NOT_EVALUABLE`
   means the procedure could not be tested. `REFERENCE_VALIDATION_FAILED` means
   it was tested and did not work. They call for different actions.
6. **Gate 3 authorises; it does not certify.** Passing means the matching
   procedure predicts held-out burden better than arbitrary controls. It does
   not mean the confirmatory estimate is unbiased, and passing every formal
   criterion does not prove the panel is matched on an unmeasured mechanism.
7. **$U_{\rm test}$ is terminal.** Report a Gate 4 failure with the matrix. Do
   not adjust and rerun; a redesign is v15.
8. **Wording is a factor.** Report the per-paraphrase table with every contrast,
   and report Gate 3b separately: the primary claim concerns the primary
   wordings, and Gate 3b concerns transport beyond them.
9. **Report pool dependence.** If identification rests on one authored family,
   say so. A sign difference between "natural only" and "natural plus shadows"
   is two counterfactual questions, not an error.
10. **Only the study profile is evidence.** `dev_fast` and `pilot` outputs are
    stamped and cannot authorize anything.
11. **Single model, single checkpoint.** Every estimate is conditional on one
    model. Cross-model validation is the first extension, not an optional
    refinement.
12. **Below the divider stays below it.**

# Alternative inference engines

`CFG.engine` is in the execution fingerprint, so a cached response generated
under one engine is never reused under another. vLLM is the largest available
speedup and a different sampler; before any vLLM result enters the primary
analysis, generate the full calibration design under both engines with matched
decoding parameters, compare the distribution of verifier outcomes per
constraint rather than the strings, require the per-constraint pass-rate
difference to fall inside the SESOI band with a task-cluster interval on the
calibration split only, and keep the primary estimand on a single engine.

Lock one hardware and software condition for the whole confirmatory dataset.
Half a confirmation on a T4 and half on a rented GPU is another experimental
factor.

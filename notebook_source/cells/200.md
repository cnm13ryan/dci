## 11. Shared inference utilities and sample-size planning

One resampling scheme serves the whole notebook: resample **task clusters**,
recompute every quantity from the resampled tasks, and read joint uncertainty
off the same draws.

Three additions over v13, all in service of "uncertainty counts against
validation".

`one_sided_upper` and `simultaneous_abs_upper` give the bounds every gate now
decides on, so no gate compares a point estimate with a tolerance.
`paired_advantage` compares the calibrated construction with random reference
sets on the *same* resample, which is the only way the comparison has a
standard error at all. `wilson_interval` bounds the verifier error rates.

Sample-size planning is rebuilt. The estimator resamples 48 task clusters, so

observations overstates it by roughly the square root of the within-task

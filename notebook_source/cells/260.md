## 17. Hard eligibility and the cheap canonical pre-screen

Hard eligibility is evaluated in two passes. The declared and empirical
relations that need no calibration run here: logical incompatibility with $A$,
entailment or duplication against **both** $A$ and $B$, generative scope, and
verifier validity from Gate M. Duplication is detected from verifier agreement
over the probe corpus rather than trusted from the registry alone.

The pre-screen is a deliberately loose filter at one wording whose only job is
to avoid paying the paraphrase multiplier on candidates nowhere near the focal
constraint. It decides nothing about eligibility and refuses to cut the pool
below twice the panel size.

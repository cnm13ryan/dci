## 21. Freeze the pseudo-focal cohort from $U_{\rm build}$

This is the correction that makes Gate 3 a test rather than a rehearsal.

In v13 the pseudo-focals and their donors were drawn from $B$'s own eligible
set, so the question was whether constraints resembling $B$ predict one
another. In v14 both come from the hard-eligible universe, whose criteria never
mention $B$: satisfiability, entailment and duplication against $A$ and $B$,
verifier validity, scope, usable measurements, adequate precision.

The cohort and the union of donors it could draw on are frozen **before**
$U_{\rm val}$ is generated, which both controls cost and makes the separation
structural: while the cohort was chosen, no measurement against $U_{\rm val}$
existed.

Note that a panel of exactly six for $B$ says nothing about how many other
constraints can each be given six donors. If too few can, the status is
`REFERENCE_VALIDATION_NOT_EVALUABLE` and confirmation is not authorized.

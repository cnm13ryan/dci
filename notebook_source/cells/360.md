## 26. CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS — the $6 \times 2$ matrix

The primary result is $\tau_{j,o}$, read as a matrix. Both orders are
co-primary; the 50/50 average is secondary and labelled, because where the two
contrasts differ in sign their average describes no condition that was run.

The reference-robust claim rule is deliberately conservative: it requires the
simultaneous max-$T$ lower bound on the least favourable admitted reference to
exceed zero at that order. Five references at $+0.20$ and one at $-0.15$ is
reference-dependent heterogeneity, not interference.

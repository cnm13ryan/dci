## 6. Verifiers

A constraint's *rule family* dispatches both its verifier and its paraphrase
templates, so a wording and the check it describes cannot drift apart.

The pathway audits are now enforced rather than asserted in prose. Whole-answer
unrelated targets are run with sentence segmentation disabled and must survive.
Bridge targets, which are sentence-scoped on purpose, must instead be invariant
to appending a neutral sentence and to reversing sentence order. $A$ and $B$ are
run through the same two probes and must **fail** them; otherwise the probes
would certify anything.

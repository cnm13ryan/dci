## 20. Candidate-pool dependence audit

Whether six admissible references exist can depend entirely on one authored
family. That is not automatically a fault — purpose-built shadows are a
legitimate reference model — but the reader is entitled to know it, because
"natural controls only" and "natural plus shadows" are different counterfactual
questions and can even differ in sign.

No confirmatory outcome is used, and the declared primary pool is not changed
in response to what this shows. Doing that would be a new protocol version.

## 22. GATE 3 — reference validation on $U_{\rm val}$

$g_1$, the six individually admissible references, is the declared primary
estimand, so $g_1$ must pass every criterion on its own. $g_0$ is computed as a
benchmark and falsification comparator and authorizes nothing. $g_2$ is fitted
and validated separately: if it fails, the stochastic secondary estimand is
withheld and the primary panel is untouched; if it passes while $g_1$ fails,
the primary experiment remains unauthorized.

Every criterion is decided on a bound rather than a point estimate, and the
random-reference comparison is paired on the same task resample. Per-reference
validation runs over $C_j \times U_{\rm val} \times \text{order}$ with
simultaneous bounds, so two references cancelling cannot be hidden in a panel
mean; the panel residual is reported as a secondary diagnostic.

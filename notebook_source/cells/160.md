## 7. Verifier tests, mutation profile, and coupling with uncertainty

v13 treated verifier coupling $\kappa_{A,Q}$ as a known constant. It is a
proportion estimated from a finite probe bank, and for the focal pair it was
undefined whenever no bank response satisfied both $A$ and $B$ — at which point
v13 silently dropped the component from matching.

v14 records the informative base responses, the mutations that actually
contributed, and the true effective denominator; estimates the standard error
by resampling the response bank; and propagates that uncertainty into
admission. Too few informative probes, or too imprecise a focal estimate, is
`PRECISION_INSUFFICIENT` — never a quiet deletion of a matching dimension.

The response bank and the mutation specification are hashed into the protocol,
because changing either changes what $\kappa$ means.

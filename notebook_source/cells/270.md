## 18. Stage 2 — calibration, paraphrase escalation, calipers

The escalation rule is a calibration-precision rule and nothing else: add two
audited wordings while measurement uncertainty still consumes more than the
permitted fraction of any caliper for a **contending** candidate, and stop at
the schedule's end. It never sees a confirmatory outcome.

Each escalation opens a new cached stage and generates only the wordings it has
not already paid for.

Calipers are then derived from outcome sensitivity against SESOI, estimated per
rotation and per order, with the conservative maximum taken where the rotations
disagree. Pool spread appears nowhere.

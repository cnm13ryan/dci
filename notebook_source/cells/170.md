## 8. Paraphrase bank and the semantic audit gate

Wordings are authored per rule family and parameterised, so a threshold is
substituted rather than retyped and cannot drift between wordings. Eight
wordings exist per family: indices 0–5 are the escalation pool, **6–7 are
permanently held out** and are evaluated empirically at Gate 3b.

Two changes from v13, both closing the same hazard.

The audit is a gate, not a label. Gate M refuses to pass until every wording
this notebook renders has been approved, and no empirical generation is
permitted before Gate M passes. The signed artifact is hashed into the protocol
provenance, because a study that changed its wordings after approval is a
different study.

The template and the signed artifact are separate files, and the template is
written only when it is absent. Rerunning this cell can no longer blank a
completed audit — which the v13 code, writing a fresh blank template to a fixed
path on every execution, could do.

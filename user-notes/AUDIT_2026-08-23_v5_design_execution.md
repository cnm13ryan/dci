# DCI-1 v5 design and execution audit — can the registered study resolve the companion-identity question?

**Audit date:** 2026-08-23 · **Scope:** the adopted-and-registered v5 preregistration (`user-notes/DCI1_preregistration_v5.md`, manifest under `dci1/evidence/dci1v5/registration/`), its implementation (`dci1/src/dci1v5/`), its development provenance (`dci1/reports/dci1v5/`), and the state of its live execution at audit time. What v4r4 itself establishes is audited separately in [`AUDIT_2026-08-23_v4r4_evidence.md`](AUDIT_2026-08-23_v4r4_evidence.md).

**Provenance.** Read-only; nothing was modified and no process was stopped. Registered rules were verified against source, and the live claims below were verified directly (signed records under the gitignored `dci1/evidence/dci1v5/`, transition logs, backend process list, and my own counts over the in-flight rows). A combined HTML rendering is published at https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f3ed981a-5c3d-412f-b040-506839766491.

## Summary

v5 exists because v4r4 answered the compositional questions (delivery loss exists; its pattern is configuration-dependent) and never reached the companion-specificity question: all six companion-specific estimands are `NOT_IDENTIFIED` study-wide. v5 is, in order of certainty, (a) a **prospective test of a rebuilt reference-construction methodology** (registered success criterion §26, judged before any composed outcome), (b) a **direct test of the primary question in a registered narrower form** — whether *however* is special among same-form exact-count marker words at matched difficulty — and (c) an **extension** of the established generic-loss result to four models with direct order estimands and the per-member L4a fix. Two facts qualify everything below: every admission threshold was fitted to v4r4 after observing it in full (§30 declares this), and **the study is executing now on a production path that deviates from the registered protocol in four places** (§4), with §32 clause 3 closing the amendment window once Gate 2.5 begins on the first replication.

## 1. Why v5 exists

**The uncertainty, stated precisely.** v4r4 answered questions 1–2 of the registered primary question (does composition reduce delivery; is it asymmetric) and never reached question 3: *is the reduction of B specific to the companion A, or what a companion of A's difficulty would produce whatever its identity?* The three estimands that carry that question — Δ_R (local matched excess), δ_diff (excess over a fitted difficulty–loss relation with A excluded), Ξ (outcome specificity) — are `NOT_IDENTIFIED` on both survivors (`final_results.json:study_level_not_identified`), because §13.4 admitted fewer than three straddling core references on three of four models and the one full-design replication (gemma-3n-e2b-it) was terminated at Gate 4 before generating a confirmatory row. This is non-identification, not a null: nothing licenses "companion identity does not matter."

**How it follows from the evidence.** The failure is structural and measured (`reference_design_development.md` F1–F6): a 24-topic point-estimate pre-screen used as a hard selector (median |Δι| 0.071; 35.7% of candidates moved more than the 0.10 caliper; straddling support 7→3, 5→0, 4→2, 4→0); ι as the sole admission coordinate (CL8:3 admitted at solo 0.974 / chance 0.932 and terminal on its own solo cell); "production class" too coarse (Δ_class +0.011 vs −0.511 for two companions of one declared class); solo-matched controls made trivial by B itself (RF11:26 0.635 → 0.969 when composed with B); support calibrated to the minimum; and an operating-point grid ending where the operating point is (m\* was the last measured rung on all four models). Behind all of it is the §4c/§4g finding that every reference family was a prefix-closed budget with chance compliance 0.52–0.89 while A's is 0.000, so "matched on solo compliance" equated companions doing all of their own work with companions doing almost none (12/12 vs 2/12).

**What v5 changes** (`DCI1_preregistration_v5.md`): the core pool inverts to production class and same form as A (Tier-1 = "use the word *thus/hence/though/whereas/indeed/namely/instead/overall* exactly q times"); ι is demoted from admission coordinate to difficulty coordinate, behind a lexicographic validity conjunction V(R) = spec ∧ sat ∧ head ∧ chance ∧ struct ∧ inter ∧ stab ∧ diff; a pre-composition interaction gate κ^B_R = P(R pass | B alone at m\*) with projection p̂ ∈ [0.10, 0.90] and |κ^B − κ| ≤ 0.25; a high-recall regime screen replacing the hard pre-screen selector; two independent 96-topic calibration splits; ≥ 5 qualifying families with two frozen backups; a near-form (Tier-2) arm; a budget-class broad control; a construct floor κ^B(m\*) ≤ 0.50 (D17); direct order contrasts (F11, L4b); a per-member L4a. §26 registers a *methodological* success criterion — ≥ 5 families passing V(R) on both splits, three used, two frozen, surviving Gate 4 without replacement — judged before any composed outcome.

**v5's own statement of what it narrowed.** §5, §7.5 and §29: "Δ_R asks whether *however* is special among discourse markers at matched difficulty. It does not ask whether A is special among companions in general; Δ_near and δ_diff carry that question, less tightly." And §30: "v5 was designed after observing the completed v4r4 study in full … This creates a real risk of overfitting the matching rule to v4r4 … Retrospective performance on v4r4 is not prospective validation."


## 2. The registered design, in brief

**Question** (§2.2): for the registered pair (A: *use "however" exactly n times*; B: *every word ≤ m letters*), per model and outcome position — (1) does composition reduce delivery; (2) is it asymmetric; (3) *is B's reduction specific to A, or what a companion of A's difficulty **and A's form** would produce whatever its identity*; (4) does any of it depend on which constraint is stated first. "And A's form" is new in v5; question 4 is newly registered.

**Estimands and ladder** (§3.2, §11): D(c←g), Λ as in v4r4; Δ_j = D(B←A) − D(B←R_j); Δ_R = D(B←A) − ⅓Σ_j D(B←R_j) over three core references; L3a Δ_R lower > 0.10, L3b lower > 0.25, L3c upper < 0.10 (the memo's refutation verdict — `strong` scope only when the near-form arm exists, D18; else `same_form_only`); δ_diff = D(B←A) minus a nine-point OLS fit (three families × three severities, A excluded), L3d direction / L3e equivalence at ±0.10; Ξ gates the *wording* of every L3 statement (`A_GENERAL_NOT_EXCLUDED` otherwise); Δ_near (three Tier-2 controls, F9) and Δ_broad (one budget companion, direction only, no claim level) replace v4r4's single Δ_class; Ord_A, Ord_B, Ord_Λ, Ord_R̄, Ord_CL form F11, the one cross-configuration family, licensing **L4b** (order dependence); **L4a** is per named member — the correction of v4r4's invalidated L4 emitter, with a required regression test reproducing the v4r4 pattern. Thresholds 0.10/0.25 are carried verbatim from `dci-construct-pass2.md` §G row 1 (14 Aug 2026) and transferred to the same-form Δ_R by D15 over the red team's R2 objection. Every L3 statement carries `REFERENCE_POOL_SAME_FORM`.

**Reference construction** (§7, §13): Tier-1 pool = eight same-form marker families ("use the word *thus/hence/though/whereas/indeed/namely/instead/overall* exactly q times", ladder q = 1…8, reserve to 12); Tier-2 = exact counts of semicolons/commas/sentences; Tier-3 = 26 carried budget families (broad-class slot only). Admission is the lexicographic conjunction V(R) = V_spec ∧ V_sat ∧ V_head ∧ V_chance ∧ V_struct ∧ V_inter ∧ V_stab ∧ V_diff, with ι demoted to the **last** component (§13.3): raw-headroom Wilson ⊂ [0.10, 0.90], lift ≥ 0.20, vacuity ≤ 0.10; chance κ ≤ 0.75 (Wilson upper ≤ 0.85); the pre-composition interaction gate κ^B_R = P(R passes | B alone at m*) with projection p̂ = κ^B + ι(1 − κ^B) ∈ [0.10, 0.90] and |κ^B − κ| ≤ 0.25; stability across two independent 96-topic calibration splits; then the ι caliper (0.10, Newcombe ∩ ±0.05, raw-solo ±0.15). Support: ≥ 8 Tier-1/2 families into calibration, ≥ 5 passing V(R), three used, two frozen backups never substituted; spectrum rungs drawn from the *same* three core families at ι_A ± 0.20; focal grid {7…14} + conditional {15, 16}; D17 construct floor κ^B(m*) ≤ 0.50 on load-zero cells; Gate 4 headroom remains terminal and non-adaptive. §26 registers the methodological success criterion (pool identified and surviving Gate 4 without replacement), recorded `MET`/`NOT_MET` before any confirmatory outcome.

**Allocation** (§16): 56 cells × 384 blocks × M = 8 per model = 172,032 confirmatory generations (688,128 across four models; 1,144,896 total including calibration, Gate 4 and the greedy run). Projected simultaneous half-widths at φ = 0.37: D 0.045, Δ_R 0.043, δ_diff 0.047, Λ 0.064, Ord 0.045 — every precondition met on the planning model. §16.4: v4r4's confirmatory effects contribute nothing to the sizing.

## 3. Can the registered design answer the core question?

**What the registered design identifies, if it executes as written.**

**Verdict on the core question.** v5 can deliver the first *identified* companion-swap estimate at k = 2 with difficulty held fixed — which no prior work or prior DCI revision has — but the identified quantity is a within-form one. It separates "*however*" from "a marker word of the same difficulty"; it separates companion **form** from companion **word** only through Δ_near; and it bounds companion **class** with one claim-less companion. The memo's refutation verdict ("< 0.10 refutes the pairwise framing entirely") is, under D15/D18, licensed by same-operator evidence plus one budget control — the red team (R1, R2) records this as adopted over objection. A `LOCAL_TIER_DISAGREE` outcome (Δ_R ≈ 0, Δ_near > 0.10: form, not word, drives interference) would be the most informative result and emits no claim level. So v5 answers a sharper, narrower question than "companion identity beyond generic difficulty", and says so.

**Design limitations that survive perfect execution.** R5 (accepted, permanent): κ^B is measured under B at m\* on one model, so the reference set — and therefore Δ_R's counterfactual — is model-specific; no cross-model scalar carries a claim. Every admission threshold (headroom band, lift floor, chance ceiling, projection ceiling 0.90 from 20 rate checks on one model, displacement bound, stability bound, support counts, the eight markers, the focal grid, D17's 0.50) was fitted to v4r4's four models (§30.1). Failure-mode composition at matched ι (instruction ignored vs attempted-and-miscounted) is unmatched by V(R); an ignored reference plausibly damages B less, which biases Δ_R toward H_pair. Marker letter-length (4–7; A's *however* is 7, the maximum B admits at m = 7) is a mundane identity mechanism neither matched nor registered as a covariate. Length remains a registered, non-identified mediator. The basis is `deterministic_measurement_validated` permanently (D9).

## 4. The executed study is not the registered one (verified in source)

v5 is executing on this machine (gitignored `dci1/evidence/dci1v5/`): both Gemma replications hold signed PASS records for `GATE_0_APPARATUS`, `REGIME_SCREEN` and `GATE_1_MEASUREMENT` at HEAD `b32c4b6` (real pinned vLLM backend; 7,848 real rows per model on the screen; `synthetic: false`), and `GATE_2_FOCAL_CALIBRATION` is generating now (e2b: 138k of 250,368 candidate rows at the time of audit). `RESEARCH_STATE.md` §4u–4v ("no model has generated a v5 response") is stale relative to the working tree. Four registered rules are dead or deviated on the production path:

1. **§12.6 coverage/expansion never executes.** `run/regime_run.py::_screen_rungs` iterates `FAMILIES`, which excludes A and B (`verifiers/constraints.py:557`), so ι_A is not measured on the pre-screen; both live regime records carry `iota_a_prescreen: None` and `coverage: {"holds": false, "reason": "iota_A is not measured on the pre-screen"}` yet `outcome: PASS`. `stages/regime.py::run_expansion` has no production caller (tests only). Consequence: no reserve rung (MK q = 9…12) can ever enter calibration; C1–C8, `REFERENCE_SUPPORT_NOT_IDENTIFIED` at design time and `CANDIDATE_RESERVE_EXHAUSTED` are unreachable.
2. **§12.7's Tier-3 priority was computed against a placeholder** `iota_a_prescreen = 0.5` (`regime_run.py:284-285`), not |ι − ι_A|.
3. **§12.7's calibration cap is not applied.** The regime record's `calibration_priority` says `n_measured: 112` (88 Tier-1/2 + 24 Tier-3; 214 dropped for budget), but `run/gates_run.py:185` builds Gate 2's candidates from `regime_record["carried_set"]` (326 rungs): the live candidate design is 250,368 rows per model (326 × 192 × 4) against the registered and manifest-bound 86,016. The 1,144,896 budget is wrong for the executed study, and the broad-class candidate pool differs from the registered one.
4. **No core-without-spectrum path.** `stages/selection.py::select_all` calls `admit_core_references` with the default `require_spectrum=True` and never falls back; a qualifying family that cannot strictly straddle ι_A is dropped *from the core set*. §8.1 registers the opposite ("a replication that cannot identify the spectrum still estimates Δ_R, Δ_j, Ξ and the reciprocal matrix"; §13.12: exhaustion sets `DIFFICULTY_SUPPORT_NOT_IDENTIFIED` and drops only the spectrum arm). This is the exact coupling that produced v4r4's F5 failure, re-implemented.
   Also: §7.2's focal extension {15, 16} is never measured (`gates_run.py` reads `FOCAL_GRID` only; a replication needing it terminates `FOCAL_RANGE_EXHAUSTED`); §12.4's adequacy statuses are raised nowhere; the environment digest is empty on every live record.

**Why timing matters.** §32 clause 3: "No component of §13 changes after Gate 2.5 begins on the first replication." The live walkers will reach Gate 2.5 on the Gemmas within hours of this audit. Items 1–3 are §12 rules that fix §13's *input*; item 4 is §13 itself. Repairing them after Gate 2.5 is an amendment against a running §13; repairing them now means superseding records from `REGIME_SCREEN` onward. Whether to pause is the researcher's decision; this audit changed nothing.

**In-flight data already strain two registered premises** (unsigned, provisional, e2b calibration rows at q = 3, n = 424 per marker; my own count): *instead* is absent from 90.6% of answers, *namely* 64.9%, *though* 48.1%, *overall* 38.0% — three or four of the eight Tier-1 markers are ignored rather than failed, which shrinks the pool v5 depends on to about four viable same-form families on this model (§13.15 needs ≥ 5 qualifying, 3 used + 2 frozen). And A and the markers *lengthen* answers (mean 109–122 words at q = 3 against an unconstrained ~105 and a 60–120 stem band), contrary to §7.4's `length_direction = neutral`; the core arm stays length-*matched* because the markers track A, but §29's "length-neutral by construction" is not what the model does.

## 5. Retrospective versus prospective

**Retrospective ≠ prospective.** Every number in `reference_design_development.md` (the 3/0/0/0 vs 4/4/2/3 attrition table, κ(R|B) separating 4/4 Gate-4 failures with 0 false alarms, the 0.75 chance ceiling from split-half |Δι|, D17's 0.50 from the κ^B(m) gap) is design fitting on v4r4 and is labelled so in the report header, §30.1, the manifest declaration and §33 check 15. Three phrasing slips exist — `reference_design_development.md:655` "validated on gemma-3n-e2b-it", the red-team header's "v5 is frozen" (Gate 5 has not run), and `RESEARCH_STATE.md` §4q stating the 4/4 separation as performance before the caveat — but no document presents a v4r4 outcome as a v5 observation.

## 6. Scientific role, and the v5 outcomes that would change the paper

**Scientific role of v5.** In order of certainty: (a) a **prospective validation of a methodological solution** developed from v4r4 — its own self-description, with §26 as the registered success criterion judged before any composed outcome; (b) a **direct test of the unresolved primary question in a registered narrower form** (Δ_R within form; Δ_near across statistic; δ_diff within marker families; Ξ within the marker matrix); (c) an **extension** of v4r4's established generic-loss result to four models with clean order estimands and the L4a fix. It is not a broader generalisation (same A, same B, same four checkpoints, carried confirmatory topics).




**What v5 result would materially change the paper's central claim.**
- Δ_R lower bound > 0.25 with Ξ > 0 and L3d, replicating per member across configurations (L4a) on at least one model → the framing moves from load to pair (H_pair), with `REFERENCE_POOL_SAME_FORM` attached: *however* is special even among matched markers.
- Strong L3c (Δ_R upper < 0.10 with the near-form arm present and Δ_near also below) plus L3e → the memo's refutation verdict at confirmatory precision: the pairwise framing is replaced by a load/form/position account — contestable under R1/R2 because the cross-form evidence is one claim-less budget companion.
- `LOCAL_TIER_DISAGREE` (Δ_R ≈ 0, Δ_near > 0.10) → interference is a property of the exact-count form, not the word: the most informative possible outcome, and one the ladder reports without a claim level.
- Any F11 interval excluding zero → position becomes a registered treatment effect (L4b), converting v4r4's descriptive sign reversal into a claim.
- `REFERENCE_SUPPORT_NOT_IDENTIFIED` on the Gemmas (plausible given the live marker data and the missing core-without-spectrum path) → the primary question is unidentified a second time, now with §26 `NOT_MET`; the paper's contribution stays methodological.

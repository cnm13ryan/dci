# DCI-1 v4r4 evidence audit — what the confirmatory study and its predecessors establish

**Audit date:** 2026-08-23 · **Scope:** the v4r4 confirmatory record, its predecessor evidence (v3, the B+R pilot, the 2026-08-20 screens, the exploratory arm), and novelty against the mandatory prior-work set. The prospective v5 design and its live execution are audited separately in [`AUDIT_2026-08-23_v5_design_execution.md`](AUDIT_2026-08-23_v5_design_execution.md).

**Provenance.** Read-only. Every number was read from a signed stage record or committed artifact, or independently recomputed from the raw scored rows under `dci1/evidence/dci1v4r4/` (all 44 published v4r4 point estimates reproduce to every printed digit; bootstrap SEs agree within Monte-Carlo error). Nothing in the repository was modified by the audit. A combined HTML rendering is published at https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f3ed981a-5c3d-412f-b040-506839766491.

## The answer

**Strongest contribution already supported.** Under a preregistered, deterministic-verifier, two-instruction design, composing an exact-count production constraint (A: *use "however" exactly n times*) with a word-length budget (B: *every word ≤ m letters*) imposes a delivery loss on at least one constraint whose 95% simultaneous interval lies wholly above zero in every surviving replication × configuration — D(A←B) = +0.39 [+0.35, +0.43] and +0.33 [+0.28, +0.37] on qwen3-4b (replicating per named member across both orders); D(A←B) = +0.12 [+0.08, +0.15] with A first and D(B←A) = +0.28 [+0.25, +0.32] with B second on gemma-3n-e4b-it — at registered precision (half-width ≤ 0.05; 384 blocks × 8 draws per replication). Which constraint pays, and whether it depends on order, differs qualitatively between the two models. That is the only confirmatory *positive* result in the repository; the one other licensed confirmatory claim, v3's equivalence for gemma-3n-e2b-it (target-first τ = +0.007 [−0.080, +0.094], |τ| < 0.10), is on a different estimand (A-side target failure against references matched on solo failure), rests on one registered draw of sixteen (the registered draw lies below the 5th percentile of alternative draws), and carries no identity-level claim because reference exchangeability failed.

**Paired with it, and more novel, is a methodological result:** the matched-companion control the whole programme rests on cannot be built from solo- or ι-matched budget constraints. This was measured, not argued, on four models — 12/12 references matched to A on solo compliance but 2/12 on chance-corrected instructed compliance; a pre-screen→calibration |Δι| median of 0.071 with 35.7% of candidates moving more than the whole caliper; Gate-4 ceilings produced by B itself; and, in the registered class arm on gemma-3n-e4b-it, two companions matched on ι to within 0.014 differing in their effect on B by −0.31 and −0.51 (simultaneous intervals wholly below −0.27). Companion structural class and focal position each move focal compliance by 0.3–0.8, an order of magnitude more than within-class companion identity (0.00–0.10 in every estimate that exists).

**How novel.** Generic compositional degradation is already established by prior work (Vasileva 2026 across 15 models and k = 1…12; Anand & Chattaraj 2026 across 3 models and k = 1…20). DCI's differentiation is not the phenomenon but the identification strategy — a directional, solo-anchored, chance-corrected, both-orders delivery-loss estimand at k = 2, with preregistered gates, masking and simultaneous intervals, and a matched-difficulty replacement design for the companion question. The closest prior work (Vasileva) concludes "not by which ones or in what order" from a symmetric co-failure statistic computed only at k ≥ 4 with no difficulty control; no mandatory paper varies companion identity at fixed count and difficulty.

**What remains for v5.** Whether the focal loss under A exceeds what a companion of A's difficulty *and form* would produce (Δ_R, δ_diff), whether any excess is B-specific (Ξ), and whether the quantities are order-dependent (Ord) — all six companion-specific estimands are `NOT_IDENTIFIED` in v4r4. The best existing evidence is exploratory and says the excess is small (Δ_R within ±0.10 on three of four models at screen scale, precision precondition met on one). v5 is principally a **prospective test of a rebuilt reference-construction procedure** (its registered §26 success criterion), which, if it succeeds, yields the first identified Δ_R — on a question v5 itself registers as narrower than the original ("is *however* special among discourse markers at matched difficulty"). v5 is executing as of this audit (both Gemma replications through Gate 1 at HEAD; Gate 2 generating), with no composed cell yet — see the companion v5 report.

## 3. Primary finding

**Narrowest accurate statement.** On two of four registered model checkpoints (Qwen3-4B with thinking disabled; Gemma-3n-E4B-it), in a preregistered two-instruction design with deterministic verifiers, instruction order balanced within block, 384 topic blocks × 8 draws per cell, and a registered claim ladder applied under a sealed analysis hash: composing *use "however" exactly n\* times* with *every word at most m\* letters* reduces delivery of at least one of the two, by +0.33 to +0.39 (A, qwen3-4b, both orders) and by +0.12 (A, e4b, A first) or +0.28 (B, e4b, B second), with simultaneous 95% intervals wholly above zero and half-widths under 0.05. On qwen3-4b the loss falls on A in both orders and B is not damaged (B gains +0.12 when stated first); on e4b both constraints lose when A is stated first and neither when B is stated first. No claim is made about compliance as a human would judge it (`scope_sentence`), about other pairs, loads above two, or other models.

**Why this is the primary candidate and not more.** It is the only result in the repository with a registered claim level (L1) emitted. It is an existence-and-magnitude result for compositional delivery loss with a directional, solo-anchored estimand at k = 2 — which no mandatory prior paper reports — but the *phenomenon* it demonstrates (degradation under composition) is already established in the literature (§6). Its contribution is therefore the estimand, the precision and the provenance, plus the order/configuration dependence it exposes, not the discovery.


## 4. Secondary findings (ranked)

1. **Methodological (primary-contribution candidate in its own right).** The matched-companion control cannot be built from solo-/ι-matched, chance-laden budget constraints, and the reasons are measured on four models (C10). Supporting confirmatory evidence: the registered Δ_class on e4b (−0.31/−0.51, CI wholly below −0.27) shows two companions matched on ι to within 0.014 differ by 0.3–0.5 in their effect on B. Exploratory support: class (0.40–0.60) and focal position (0.29–0.35) each dominate within-class identity (0.05–0.10) in the matrix screen. Status: ESTABLISHED as a property of this constraint vocabulary; it is design evidence, not an effect estimate.
2. **Configuration (order) dependence.** Registered per-member classifications differ across configurations on e4b; post-hoc block-paired contrasts on the confirmatory rows are large and far from zero (Ord_B −0.28, Ord_Λ −0.43 on e4b; Ord_B −0.11, Ord_A +0.06 on qwen). SUPPORTED_DESCRIPTIVELY; unregistered test; v5 registers it.
3. **An order-robust trade-off on qwen3-4b** (A sacrificed, B unharmed) against a first-mentioned-constraint pattern on e4b — qualitative model heterogeneity, confounded with operating point (B:12/n\*=2 vs B:10/n\*=4). SUPPORTED_DESCRIPTIVELY.
4. **Exploratory bound on the companion-identity excess.** Every Δ_R-type quantity ever estimated in this vocabulary lies in −0.02…+0.10 on the Gemmas and ≈ 0 (with D(B←A) ≤ 0) on the Qwens; never near 0.25. The v3 A-side Δ^A (+0.064/+0.074, survives length matching) is the best-provenanced number (registered diagnostic, 384 blocks). EXPLORATORY / registered-diagnostic; useful supporting observation.
5. **Mechanism diagnostics** (exact-count overshoot; register capture by the first-stated constraint; clause-chain affordance in the exploratory arm). EXPLORATORY; insufficient for a paper claim beyond "hypotheses for a factorial".


## 5. Claims to discard or qualify

- **"A specifically interferes with B" / any pair-specific claim.** Δ_R, Δ_j, δ_diff, Ξ are NOT_IDENTIFIED; L3 was unreachable in every configuration. The pilot's +0.34/+0.38 is unresolved with both bounds (+0.095/+0.068 forced) and collinear with a five-fold length difference; citing it alone misreads the record (`CHANGES_SCIENTIFIC.md`, 2026-08-20).
- **"Companion identity does not matter" / "generic load refuted."** Non-identification is not a null. The screen's equivalence reading holds at percentile precision on three models but the registered precision precondition is met on one, the heterogeneity qualifier fires on both Gemmas, and the references were matched on the wrong coordinate. The registered vocabulary for this is INDETERMINATE at 0.10, not L3c.
- **"The loss is directionally asymmetric" (L2).** Withheld everywhere by the registered gate; on e4b the Λ reversal is a first-mentioned effect. Say "which constraint pays depends on order" instead.
- **"Replicates across configurations" (L4) on e4b.** The emitted field is invalidated (`l4_registered_reading.agrees_with_the_emitted_field: false`); the registered reading is configuration dependence. Only D(A←B) on qwen3-4b replicates per member.
- **"A is more disruptive than its difficulty predicts."** The +0.067/+0.118 difficulty residuals are screen-scale, interval-free, on a raw-solo coordinate with budget companions; and Ξ ≤ 0 on the same models says A damages references as much as B.
- **"Model heterogeneity" as an estimate.** n = 2, and the study cannot separate model identity from operating point.
- **"Four models."** Two terminated at Gate 4 before any confirmatory row (`COMPOSED_HEADROOM_FAILURE`, one on a solo cell already determined at Gate 2); terminal statuses carry no evidence about the effect.
- **The exploratory arm's findings** (τ up to +0.30, "effect is real but not pair-specific", "order is a treatment", "structural zero" cross-model) are a different constraint vocabulary on 20 topics and are stamped `EXPLORATORY_NO_EVIDENCE`; the src/dci v19 confirmatory run was never executed.
- **Withdrawn numbers not to quote:** pilot ≥20-word restriction (+0.348/+0.360); matrix-screen length residual ±0.010; "intervals disjoint" for D(B←CR6:7) vs D(B←A) (still present uncorrected at `matrix_screen_v4/REPORT.md` lines 470, 531); "0.208–0.659" (a range of minima; true range 0.208–0.870).

**Discovered while reading:** Zeng et al. 2025, *Order Matters: position bias in multi-constraint instruction following* (arXiv 2502.17204; not cited in the repository) reports systematic order effects with a difficulty-distribution index ("hard-to-easy" orders perform better). Read at abstract level only. It means DCI's order-dependence observation is not novel as a phenomenon; DCI's contribution there would be the k = 2, pair-level, registered contrast (v5 §11.7), not the existence of position bias.

**Where DCI's differentiation actually lies.** Not in discovering compositional degradation (Vasileva; Anand & Chattaraj) and not in discovering order sensitivity (Zeng; MOSAIC). It lies in (i) a directional, solo-anchored, chance-measured delivery-loss estimand at k = 2 with both orders inside every block, and (ii) an identification strategy for companion identity versus difficulty — matched-difficulty replacement, a difficulty-response fit with the target excluded, reciprocal specificity, a class/form control arm, preregistered gates and masking — together with the demonstration that the naive version of (ii) fails for measurable structural reasons. No mandatory paper varies companion identity at fixed count and difficulty; the one that addresses pair effects (Vasileva) does so with a symmetric co-failure statistic outside the k = 2 regime. The honest priority statement is "no prior work in this set identifies companion effects beyond difficulty", not "first to show interference".


## 7. Paper-level synthesis

**Strongest primary contribution currently supported.** Two things that belong together: (1) a preregistered, verifier-defined demonstration that composing two verifiable instructions at load two produces large, directional delivery loss on at least one of them — +0.33…+0.39 on A in both orders on qwen3-4b; +0.28 on B when stated second on gemma-3n-e4b-it — with simultaneous intervals above zero at half-widths ≤ 0.05, the loss pattern being configuration- and model-dependent; and (2) the measured demonstration that the matched-companion control required to ask "beyond difficulty" cannot be built from solo- or ι-matched, chance-laden budget constraints, because class and position each move focal compliance by 0.3–0.8 while within-class companion identity moves it by ≤ 0.1 in every estimate that exists. The paper's defensible headline is therefore methodological: *what it takes to identify companion identity beyond difficulty, and why existing designs (including this programme's own earlier revisions and the co-failure statistics in prior work) do not.*

**Strongest secondary contributions.** Configuration (order) dependence as a first-class treatment effect (registered per-member classifications flip on e4b; post-hoc block-paired Ord_B = −0.28, Ord_Λ = −0.43); the order-robust A-sacrifice trade-off on qwen3-4b versus the first-mentioned-constraint pattern on e4b (descriptive, operating-point-confounded); the registered Δ_class contrasts showing that ι-matching is blind to form (e4b −0.31/−0.51; qwen +0.01/+0.04 against A's structural twin); and the exploratory bound that every Δ_R-type excess ever estimated in this vocabulary lies in −0.02…+0.10 and never near 0.25.

**Central unresolved question.** Whether D(B←A) exceeds ⅓Σ_j D(B←R_j) — the loss under companions matched to A on difficulty *and form* — by more than the 0.10 generic-load threshold, whether any excess is B-specific (Ξ), and whether it is a property of the marker word, the exact-count form, or the constraint class. Nothing confirmatory speaks to it; the exploratory evidence points to "small", but INDETERMINATE at 0.10 is the honest registered reading.

**v5's scientific role.** Principally a prospective test of the rebuilt reference-construction procedure (§26), which if met yields the first identified Δ_R at k = 2 — on a question v5 itself registers as narrower ("is *however* special among discourse markers at matched difficulty") — plus registered order contrasts and a four-model extension of the generic-loss result. Its ability to deliver even that is currently conditional on four registered rules that the live code does not execute as written, and on a Tier-1 pool that the in-flight data show is smaller than registered (companion v5 report, §§3–4).

**What v5 result would materially change the paper's central claim.**
- Δ_R lower bound > 0.25 with Ξ > 0 and L3d, replicating per member across configurations (L4a) on at least one model → the framing moves from load to pair (H_pair), with `REFERENCE_POOL_SAME_FORM` attached: *however* is special even among matched markers.
- Strong L3c (Δ_R upper < 0.10 with the near-form arm present and Δ_near also below) plus L3e → the memo's refutation verdict at confirmatory precision: the pairwise framing is replaced by a load/form/position account — contestable under R1/R2 because the cross-form evidence is one claim-less budget companion.
- `LOCAL_TIER_DISAGREE` (Δ_R ≈ 0, Δ_near > 0.10) → interference is a property of the exact-count form, not the word: the most informative possible outcome, and one the ladder reports without a claim level.
- Any F11 interval excluding zero → position becomes a registered treatment effect (L4b), converting v4r4's descriptive sign reversal into a claim.
- `REFERENCE_SUPPORT_NOT_IDENTIFIED` on the Gemmas (plausible given the live marker data and the missing core-without-spectrum path) → the primary question is unidentified a second time, now with §26 `NOT_MET`; the paper's contribution stays methodological.

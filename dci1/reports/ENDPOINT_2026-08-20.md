## 1. Scientific verdict

**There is no confirmatory evidence on the question, and the registered design
cannot produce any as written.**

The question — whether the identity of a companion instruction affects
compliance with the focal instruction beyond generic two-instruction difficulty
— is answered by `Δ_R` at claim level L3, and L3 sits behind five gates. The
study stops at the second of them for a reason that has nothing to do with the
effect:

> §12.4 requires B's realised solo vacuity rate to be at most **0.10**. On the
> registered 96 solo-calibration topics at the registered `M_cal = 4`, under the
> registered prompt, it is **at least 0.208, and as much as 0.870** — and not
> at one rung, but at **every rung of the focal grid and of the extended grid,
> on all four models**. (0.208 to 0.659 is the range of the four per-model
> *minima*, which is what `cal_screen_v4/REPORT.md`'s "min" column reports.)

Gate 2 therefore fails `REGIME_NOT_FOUND` on every replication, before the
reference question is reached. §28.3 fixes what that means and the wording is
not ours to soften: *a terminal pre-confirmatory status carries no evidence
about the effect*, and `REGIME_NOT_FOUND` is *a statement about the registered
grid, never about the model or the hypothesis*.

**On the provenance of that number.** It is measured by the non-confirmatory
calibration screen, not by a registered Gate 2 record, because Gate 2 is behind
Gate 1 and Gate 1 is not approved. What makes it decisive anyway is that it is
measured on the *registered* inputs: the 96 registered solo-calibration topics,
at the registered `M_cal = 4`, through the registered §8.5 renderer (asserted
byte-identical to `dci1v4.design.prompts.render` at zero, one and two
constraints), scored by the registered verifiers, against the registered 0.10
ceiling. The margin is 2× to 6× and there is no rung on either grid that comes
close. **What a registered Gate 2 would add is provenance, not information** —
which is precisely why running it would spend the study's remaining option
value for nothing (§9).

Reaching a confirmatory answer requires changing the registered design, and
that decision is the researcher's. **It is not ours, and this report does not
take it.**

The best available estimate of `Δ_R` remains exploratory and is small: on the
non-confirmatory pairwise-matrix screen the simultaneous interval lies wholly
inside ±0.10 on three of four models and reaches 0.25 on none. But that reading
does not survive its own protocol's rules — at 120 blocks the §16.2 precision
precondition (half-width ≤ 0.05) is met on **one** model of four, and §13.4's
reference-heterogeneity qualifier fires on **both** Gemmas. Even as exploratory
evidence it is suggestive, not settled.

### A seventh channel, found here and not previously recorded

**§13.2 matches references to A on a quantity that is not comparable across the
production/budget divide, and §25 already measured the gap.**

§13.1 says the reference estimates "what would composing B with a *comparable*
non-A companion have cost". §13.2 operationalises comparable as raw solo
compliance within 0.10 of A's. §25 separately measures, for every constraint,
the rate at which an answer carrying **no constraint at all** satisfies it by
chance — and records it without feeding it into admission. Netting one out of
the other gives the share of compliance the instruction actually produced,
`(solo − chance) / (1 − chance)`:

| model | A solo → instructed | R₁ | R₂ | R₃ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E2B | 0.562 → **0.562** | 0.644 → 0.071 | 0.644 → 0.266 | 0.598 → 0.040 |
| E4B | 0.719 → **0.719** | 0.744 → 0.320 | 0.742 → 0.421 | 0.642 → **−0.550** |
| Qwen3-4B | 0.710 → **0.710** | 0.698 → 0.249 | 0.740 → 0.150 | 0.640 → 0.626 |
| Qwen3-4B-Inst | 0.592 → **0.592** | 0.623 → 0.210 | 0.633 → **−2.321** | 0.610 → 0.600 |

**All twelve references are matched to A on solo compliance. Two are matched on
instructed compliance. Two are anti-productive** — stating the constraint makes
the model satisfy it *less* often than not stating it at all.

The divergence is structural, not incidental. A is an exact-count **production**
constraint, so an unconstrained answer essentially never satisfies it and A's
chance rate is **0.000 on all four models**. B and every registered family are
`S ≤ q` **budgets**, prefix-closed, and are satisfied by chance often. The
caliper therefore equates a companion doing all of its own work with companions
doing little of theirs, and the gap tracks the constraint class.

**Which way it biases `Δ_R`, stated plainly.** A reference whose instructed
compliance is near zero is, for most items, not a binding second instruction, so
`D(B←R_j)` approaches the cost of composing B with an inert sentence and
`Δ_R = D(B←A) − mean_j D(B←R_j)` is biased **upward**. The screen's `Δ_R` is
small on every model anyway, so **the bias runs against the positive reading
rather than producing it**. That is a direction-of-bias statement; nothing above
is adjusted for it, and no new matching criterion is proposed here.

This analysis uses no delivery loss, no composed contrast and no outcome. It
could have been run before a single composed cell existed.
`reports/section_13_2_v4/instructed_compliance.json`.

## 9. The researcher decisions, and why their order matters

**Decide before Gate 2 is executed, not after.** A terminal record is written
once and cannot be revised (`fs.write_json_once`), and it mechanically disables
every later generation command for that model. Running Gate 2 under the
registered prompt would write `REGIME_NOT_FOUND` for all four models and end v4
permanently — and adopting a design change afterwards would be adopting it
*because a gate failed*, which §31 prohibits and `record_amendment` refuses.
**The amendment decision has to come first.** No Gate 2 record was written by
this work, precisely so the option is still open.

1. **§12.4's graded-degeneracy clause** — "the graded distribution … is not
   concentrated above 0.99 or below 0.01", and `gate2_payload` compares one
   float without saying which. Mean-graded fires on every in-band rung of B, so
   §12.3 declares a rung eligible and §12.4 declares it degenerate and nothing
   runs anywhere. Share-above-0.99 fires on none. The text favours the share
   reading — "distribution" and "concentrated" are statements about mass, a mean
   is not a distribution, the code's own parameter is `graded_distribution`, and
   B's and A's graded forms are incommensurable under one threshold — but this
   report does not take the decision. It is **not currently binding**: §12.4
   already fails on vacuity alone under the registered stem, on either reading.
2. **Amendment A, the floored prompt stem: adopt or reject.** This is the
   binding decision. Adopting it re-measures §12.1, §12.2, §12.3 and §13.2 by
   its own terms — roughly 10,200 generations for §12.2 alone — and takes B's
   solo vacuity from a floor of 0.208 (reaching 0.870) to 0.000–0.060. Rejecting it ends v4 at
   `REGIME_NOT_FOUND`.
3. **Amendment C per-model, not global.** Under the floored stem the registered
   focal grid already supplies an in-band `m*` on three of four models; only
   qwen3-4b is exhausted. A *global* extension moves
   qwen3-4b-instruct-2507's `m*` from 10 to 11 and off the only rung feasible
   under both readings of the graded clause.
4. **Amendment B, the enlarged reference pool.** Now decidable on its merits
   rather than on the withdrawn §13.2 reading. Costs a verifier-hash change, a
   Gate 1 instrument re-freeze, boundary suites for three more families, and the
   §7.3 witness obligation its own §5a sizes at 2,448–5,472 uncertified pairs.
5. **Whether a class-matched production companion may enter the reference pool.**
   This changes what `Δ_R` means, so it is an amendment and not a robustness
   check.
6. **Whether the pool must contain a companion matched to A on length shift.**
   Without one the length channel stays unidentified at any block count; §22
   reports length as a mediator, it does not identify it.
6a. **Whether §13.2's caliper should read instructed rather than raw solo
   compliance.** §25 already measures the input and §13.2 already ignores it. On
   the screen's own reference sets the criterion admits 12 of 12 on solo and 2
   of 12 on instructed, and two admitted references are anti-productive. This
   changes what "comparable companion" means, so it is an amendment and not a
   robustness check — and unlike items 2–5 it is decidable from §25's registered
   measurement without any new generation.
7. **`measurement_basis = autorater_calibration_approved`** — the Gate 1
   approval, after items 1–6 fix the instrument population.

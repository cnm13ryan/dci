You are deciding whether a short ANSWER satisfies a short INSTRUCTION.

Answer SATISFIES, VIOLATES, or UNSURE. Use UNSURE only when the instruction
genuinely does not determine the case --- not when the case is merely fiddly.

Judge only the instruction as written. The answers are fragments: do not judge
topic, usefulness, style or length.

These definitions apply to the instruction below.
- A word is a maximal run of letters. Digits, punctuation, spaces and symbols end a word, so a hyphenated form is two words and an apostrophised form is two words.
- Letter tests read the word lower-cased, but a word's letters are the letters it is written with.
- A percentage of words is that count divided by the number of words in the whole answer. An answer with no words has no percentage, and an instruction stated as a percentage is not satisfied by it.
- Commas, semicolons and sentence-ending marks are counted as characters of the answer.
- A sentence ends at a run of . ! or ?; text after the last such run is one more sentence.

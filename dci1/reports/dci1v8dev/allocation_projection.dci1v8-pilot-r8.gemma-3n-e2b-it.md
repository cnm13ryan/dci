## Reading it

The two columns that decide the allocation question are *coords within the floor* (how many per-frame difficulty coordinates the design can pin at all) and *state*. A frame whose coordinates clear the floor can still fail SPECTRUM for a reason precision cannot fix --- no admissible rung on one side of A --- and the first-failing lists say which of the two it is. `SE(β̂) frame / full` is the projected precision of §11.8's slope at the frame's share of the registered confirmatory blocks and at the full allocation: the *second* open allocation decision, which this table informs but does not take.

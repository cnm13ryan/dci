## Visual grammar, identical across Figures 2–4

| Encoding | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Colour** | the companion instruction present in the composed cell: blue `#0173B2` = A, rust `#B4590A` = B, teal `#029E73` = the matched reference pool; neutral ink where the mark is a contrast between two arms |
| **Marker shape** | what the mark is: ● a loss measured on B, ■ a loss measured on A, ⬢ the mean loss over the three matched references, ◆ a contrast between two composed arms |
| **Marker fill** | the measured instruction's position: filled = stated first, open = stated second |

Colour is always redundant with shape and fill, so nothing depends on colour.
The palette passes all six checks of the `dataviz` skill's validator on an
all-pairs run against a light surface (lightness band, chroma floor, CVD
separation, normal-vision floor, contrast) — grayscale renders are in
`_check_gray_*.png`.

## Design decisions that were made to avoid a misleading figure

1. **Figure 2 shows the arithmetic bound.** For two configurations
   (`qwen3-4b` and `qwen3-4b-instruct-2507`, B stated first) B's compliance sits
   at ≈0.90 in *both* composed arms, so Δ_R could not have exceeded +9.9 pp
   whatever the model did — essentially the +10 pp threshold itself. Plotting the
   interval alone would imply the threshold was reachable. The hatched apron and
   stop bar mark the limit (v7 §13.14 reading 3).

2. **Figure 4 panel A groups by prompt, not by "outcome position".**
   `D(B←A)|outcome_first` and `D(A←B)|outcome_first` are read from **different
   prompts** (B+A and A+B respectively) — the misreading v5 §11.1 warns about.
   Rows are therefore prompt orders, so the two marks of a row are the two
   instructions measured in one and the same prompt, and exactly one of them is
   position-confounded. The audit asserts both properties.

3. **The reference mean is drawn without an interval.** `mean_j D(B←R_j)` is a
   derived average of three F2 members, not a registered estimand, so it carries
   no simultaneous interval. Figure 3 draws only `D(B←A)`'s interval and the
   legend and caption say why.

4. **No "equivalent to zero" language anywhere.** Δ_R's registered reading is
   "upper bound below +0.10", not equality with zero — and in three of the eight
   configurations the interval lies entirely *below* zero, which the figure shows
   as a resolved direction (`A_COMPARATIVELY_BETTER`), not as a null.

5. **Nothing unavailable is drawn as zero.** `Δ_near`, `δ_difficulty`, the
   difficulty-spectrum fit and the six spectrum losses are `NOT_IDENTIFIED` on
   every lane and appear in no figure. `Ord_CL` is identified only on the two
   Gemma lanes and is omitted rather than shown as a gap.

6. **The broad-class comparison is not a main-paper figure.** Beyond the
   registered `COMPANION_CLASS_NOT_MATCHED` status, the post-hoc confirmatory
   coordinate shows the broad-class companion was not difficulty-matched at all
   (`../dossier/posthoc_confirmatory_difficulty.csv`). It stays in the appendix.

7. **Models are never pooled** and repeat lanes are never plotted as independent
   observations; both are enforced by the audit.

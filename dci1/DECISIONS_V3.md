artifact exists** (all dated 2026-08-17, pre-registration of the study
tree). The sole scientific authority is
`user-notes/DCI-1_preregistration_v3.md`; the anti-regression specification
is `user-notes/DCI-1_v2-to-v3_changelog.md`. Nothing below alters a
registered scientific rule; each entry either fixes a manifest value the
preregistration delegates to registration, or records the executable
reading of a construction the preregistration describes in prose.
